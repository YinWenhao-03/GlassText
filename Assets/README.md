# 应用图标

使用内置 image_gen 生图工具生成。当前采用 AppIcon-Glass.png，并转换为 AppIcon.icns。早期 AppIcon.png 是未采用的候选，不参与应用打包。

最终提示词：Create a polished premium macOS app icon for GlassText, a transparent floating text utility. Luminous frosted glass, rounded-square tile, icy pale blue and lavender rim refraction, white highlights, restrained translucent depth. Central symbol: transparent rectangular floating window with three crisp white text lines of decreasing length hovering in front, one line extending beyond its boundary. No alphabet letters, paper folds, pushpins, arrows, Chinese characters or captions. Square composition, generous margin, transparent exterior.
